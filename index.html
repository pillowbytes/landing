Monstera Plant Guide

Low Maintenance

This tropical plant is definitely a household favorite, so here are some tips from Jade Adams on how to care for a monstera deliciosa plant so it thrives in your home.

Easy for beginners

Monsteras come in many different varieties (monstera deliciosa variegata, monstera obliqua, monstera borsigiana, monstera dubia, monstera adansonii etc.) and are fairly easy to care for.

How Big Does it Get?

Monstera Plants, specially the Deliciosas can get huge! In the wild its leaves can easily grow more than 1 meter long!
